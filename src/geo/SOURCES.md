## How second-level areas were placed

geoBoundaries publishes LGAs, sub-counties and municipalities as shapes without a parent code. The build assigns each to the state, county or province that contains most of its boundary points (each point pulled 5% towards the shape's centre so shared borders do not tie), and stores the mean of its largest ring as an approximate centre (rounded to 0.01°, about 1 km). The build fails unless every Nigerian state has its constitutional LGA count, and it spot-checks Kenya (Kisumu Central → Kisumu, Westlands → Nairobi, Malindi → Kilifi) and South Africa (Johannesburg → Gauteng, eThekwini → KwaZulu-Natal, Cape Town → Western Cape, Polokwane → Limpopo).

Corrections applied on top of the sources, all in `scripts/geo/build.mjs`:
- `GH-BA` (Brong-Ahafo) is dropped: it was split into Bono, Bono East and Ahafo in 2018 and withdrawn from ISO 3166-2.
- Display names: `NG-FC` "Federal Capital Territory", `KE-30` "Nairobi", `KE-05` "Elgeyo-Marakwet", `KE-39` "Taita-Taveta", `ZA-KZN` "KwaZulu-Natal", `ZA-NW` "North West". Codes are unchanged.
- Capitalisation after apostrophes in GRID3 names ("Jema'A" → "Jema'a"). GRID3 spellings are otherwise kept (Oturkpo, Port-Harcourt, Emuoha); curated language lists accept both spellings.

## Curated languages by area (`curated.ts`)

National CLDR figures would mislead below country level (Kano mothers are unlikely to speak Yoruba because 13% of Nigerians do), so every Nigerian state, Kenyan county and Ghanaian region, and every South African province, has a hand-built list, with some LGA-level lists in Nigeria (Delta, Rivers, Cross River, Edo, Benue, Borno, Kaduna, Lagos). Each entry carries its citation:

- Nigeria: Ethnologue, *Languages of the World*, 27th ed. (2024), Nigeria; R. Blench, *An Atlas of Nigerian Languages*, 4th ed. (2019); Wikipedia state articles; Nigeria DHS 2018 (ethnicity by state).
- Kenya: Kenya National Bureau of Statistics, 2019 Kenya Population and Housing Census, Vol. IV (distribution by socio-economic characteristics, ethnic affiliation by county); Ethnologue (2024), Kenya.
- Ghana: Ghana Statistical Service, 2021 Population and Housing Census, General Report Vol. 3C; Ethnologue (2024), Ghana.
- South Africa: Statistics South Africa, Census 2022, first language by province.

These are orderings, not measured shares, and most are marked `approximate: true`. The UI can show `languageSource(country, region)` under "Why these languages?". Corrections from health workers are welcome.

## Malaria (`malaria.ts`)

Country-level defaults for places with no curated location profile: WHO *World Malaria Report 2024* (countries with indigenous cases in 2023, and IPTp coverage tables for countries with IPTp-SP policy) and WHO certifications of elimination to 2025. IPTp follows the WHO *Guidelines for malaria* (IPTp-SP in areas of moderate to high transmission in Africa). Notes on focal transmission (South Africa, Botswana, Namibia, Eswatini, Ethiopia, Eritrea, Rwanda) come from the same report's country profiles. A curated location profile always wins.
