# Clinical decisions for Elizabeth's review (before any real patient use)

Each item is a judgement the rules engine makes today. Mark agree / change / unsure.

1. Sickle cell disease in pregnancy (Nigeria): plan gives folic acid 5 mg daily (believed national practice) and states the conflict with WHO 2025, which says 400 µg for women taking SP. Needs the advisory board's ruling.
2. Blood pressure thresholds: 140/90 = urgent referral; 160/110, or 140/90 with headache, blurred vision or protein in urine = emergency. Follows ISSHP/WHO usage; WHO primary text not yet retrieved.
3. Wankan jego graded "neutral" (not harmful): keep warmth and rest, limit kanwa salt, treat breathlessness after birth as urgent. Based on the PEACE registry (406 patients), which found no link with peripartum cardiomyopathy.
4. Kenya IPTp: applied only in malaria-endemic counties; the county list is an assumption.
5. Depression screening (EPDS): Nigeria uses 10 (positive screen, follow-up within a week) and 12 (urgent referral); Kenya and Ghana use 10 and 13. Any self-harm answer = same-day emergency.
6. Doses: iron 60 mg with folic acid 400 µg daily; aspirin 75 mg from 12 weeks for high pre-eclampsia risk.
7. Cord care: chlorhexidine gel in Nigeria; clean, dry care in Kenya and Ghana.
8. Growth faltering: a simple weight-trend check, not yet WHO z-score charts.
9. Kenya immunisation schedule taken from a secondary source.
10. CHEW scope: where the 2015 Standing Orders are unclear, the plan says "refer" rather than "give".
11. Plain-language mother text: 191 templates to read for tone and accuracy (src/clinical/motherText.ts).
